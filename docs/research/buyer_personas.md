# Orkelya Buyer Personas & Market Positioning

## Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)
Orkelya provides high-throughput AI agents, workflow automation, and custom software systems for operations-heavy B2B businesses.

### Persona 1: Founder / CEO of B2B Service Agency / Software Company
- **Pain Point:** Overhead cost of manual operations, slow lead follow-up, fragmented communication between sales, CRM, and fulfillment.
- **Goal:** Scale revenue without linearly increasing headcount.
- **Content Trigger:** Demonstrations showing end-to-end automated workflows (e.g. WhatsApp lead → AI qualification → Calendar booking → CRM update).

### Persona 2: Operations Lead / COO
- **Pain Point:** Repetitive manual data entry, human error in data handoffs, disjointed tools (Excel, Email, WhatsApp, CRM).
- **Goal:** Predictable, error-free operational pipelines.
- **Content Trigger:** Before/After comparisons, bottleneck breakdowns, architecture diagrams.

### Persona 3: Technical Lead / CTO
- **Pain Point:** Skepticism towards "AI hype" and brittle "no-code chatbots" that lack tool access or enterprise security.
- **Goal:** Robust, API-first orchestration with human-in-the-loop controls.
- **Content Trigger:** Architecture diagrams, code cards, RAG & tool-calling technical breakdowns.
